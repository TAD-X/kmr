# The Kitchen Mod Reborn 0.6.0

## What's new: the Oven
- **Craft it** with iron, coal and flint & steel (`III / ICI / IFI`). It faces you when placed.
- **Sneak + empty hand** opens and closes the hatch.
- **While open:** right-click with food to put it in (4 slots, up to 4 of one kind in each, so 16 of one kind fit); an empty hand takes out a stack. Nothing can be added while closed. You can see the food inside through the hatch.
- **Fuel:** right-click with anything that burns (coal, charcoal, planks, lava bucket...) while open. One fuel item runs one cook cycle.
- **Close it** with fuel and food in it to cook. After 20 seconds the food is cooked; after 30 seconds it all burns to Burnt Meat. Smoke comes out of the front once it's done (and flames when it burns). The oven looks lit while cooking.
- **Opening the oven mid-cook** stops the cycle: the timer resets and the fuel is used up (as in the original). Food that has already cooked stays cooked.
- **What it cooks:** raw roast beef, raw chicken fillet and raw bacon, plus anything a smoker or furnace cooks into food (porkchop, potato, fish...). The timings are in `data/kitchen/kitchen/oven_config/default.json`.
- Breaking an oven drops the food.

## Requires
- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer
- Fabric API 0.166.0+26.3

**Both the server and all clients must use the same jar.**
