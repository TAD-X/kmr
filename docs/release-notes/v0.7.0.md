# The Kitchen Mod Reborn 0.7.0

## What's new: the Frying Pan and jam
- **Craft the pan** from five iron ingots (`III / II_`). It only exists on top of an oven (any oven, lit or not) and takes the oven's facing; take the oven away and the pan comes off.
- **Frying:** right-click with raw bacon or raw chicken fillet. After 15 seconds it is done: the raw item turns into the cooked one and smoke rises. Empty hand takes it out. (Like the original, fried food never burns. You can take the raw food back out before it is done.)
- **Jam:** put in sugared strawberry slices, apple slices or peanuts. The pan fills in three stages as it cooks, in the jam's colour, and smokes when it is ready. Right-click the finished jam with an **empty jam jar** to fill it with 6 servings.
- **Jam jars** now show how full they are and are tinted with the jam colour. Use a jar on a cutting board to put a jam layer on bread (one serving each); when the last serving is used it becomes an empty jar again. Strawberry jam, apple jam and peanut butter all work.
- Bubbling and sizzling sounds while the pan cooks.
- The pan item shows in your hand and the inventory like a block.
- Timing is data: `data/kitchen/kitchen/pan_config/default.json` (300 ticks), and the `kitchen:pan` recipe type holds the recipes.

## Requires
- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer
- Fabric API 0.166.0+26.3

**Both the server and all clients must use the same jar.**
