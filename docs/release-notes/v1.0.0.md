# The Kitchen Mod Reborn 1.0.0

The original feature set of MrSpring's Kitchen Mod, rebuilt for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric.

## What's new in 1.0.0
- **Kitchen Cabinet:** the original model, a real 27-slot container that opens like a barrel, with comparator output. Quartz over planks.
- **Tiles:** the original checkerboard block (black dye, bone meal and clay make two), plus tile slabs and stairs.
- **Sounds:** knife chopping, the oven door, sizzling in the pan, filling a jar.
- **Translations:** the original's translations for 23 languages, for everything that still exists in the port. Anything missing shows in English.
- Frying pan held in your off-hand is now mirrored properly in first and third person.

## Everything since 0.4.0
- **Plate (0.5):** stack up to 32 items on it, with sandwiches shown at board size, and pick it up with its contents kept. Plates now render in your hand, on the ground and in frames; rules stop plates being used as storage (unstackable items one at a time, no shulker boxes, no plates on plates).
- **Oven (0.6):** craft it, open and close it with an animated hatch, load food and a fuel item, cook in 20 seconds, burn in 30. Cooks bacon, roast beef, chicken fillet and anything a furnace or smoker can cook.
- **Frying pan and jam (0.7):** fries bacon and chicken, boils strawberry, apple and peanut jam in three visible stages, collected with an empty jar. Jars show how full they are and are tinted per jam, and work on the cutting board.
- Many fixes to how sandwiches, plates and pans look in hand, on the ground and in the inventory.

## Requires
- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer
- Fabric API 0.166.0+26.3

**Both the server and all clients must use the same jar.**

See [docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/TAD-X/kmr/blob/main/docs/FEATURES.md) for how everything works and how it differs from the original.
